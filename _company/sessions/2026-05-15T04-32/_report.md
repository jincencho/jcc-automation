2. **Marketing Agent**: Responsible for creating content, managing projects, and tracking performance metrics such as clicks, views, and conversion rates. Uses various tools like Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and SEO tools for optimizing marketing campaigns.

3. **Researcher Agent**: Conducts research on industry trends, competitors, and keywords to help shape marketing strategies. Utilizes resources like YouTube Ads Library, YouTube Trends, Google Keyword Planner, Awario, SEMRush, and Tubular Insights for data gathering.

4. **Developer Agent**: Develops custom tools and solutions for the system as needed. May work with front-end and back-end technologies depending on the task at hand.

The system is designed to regularly review company goals, recent decisions, and previously completed tasks (memory) to determine the most valuable single task for each agent to focus on at any given time. This ensures that the agents are always working on high-priority tasks and avoiding redundancy.
