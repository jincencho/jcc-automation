# Marketing Strategy for Home Appliance Brand JCC

## Background

The home appliance brand, JCC, operates an automated marketing system managed by a team of artificial intelligence agents. Each agent has their role and personal goals, collaborating to execute various tasks and projects. In this scenario, the focus is on Research and Design for the home appliance market.

## Objective

The objective is to identify key trends, keywords, and hashtags in the home appliance market and create visually appealing designs that align with JCC's brand identity and marketing strategies.

## Task Details

1. **Research**: The Researcher agent will gather data related to market trends, competitor analysis, and popular products in the home appliance industry from Naver Blog (a Korean search engine).
2. **Design**: Based on the findings from the Researcher agent's analysis, the Designer agent will create designs such as videos, graphics, and website layouts that effectively communicate key insights and marketing strategies for JCC.
3. **Collaboration**: The Researcher and Designer agents will collaborate to ensure consistent results that align with JCC's brand identity and guidelines.

## Expected Output

1. High-quality visual elements such as videos, graphics, and website layouts supporting JCC's marketing efforts in the home appliance industry.
2. A detailed report summarizing key findings from the Researcher agent's analysis, including trends, keywords, and hashtags.

## Tools & Resources

1. Video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro)
2. Graphic design tools (e.g., Adobe Illustrator, Sketch)
3. Web design software (e.g., Adobe XD, Figma)
4. Brand guidelines and assets provided by JCC
5. Research tools such as Ahrefs, SEMrush, or SimilarWeb (if available)
6. Word processor or presentation software for reporting (Microsoft Office/Google Docs)

## Timeline

Varies depending on the complexity of the designs and the number of elements to be created. It may take several days to a week or more for larger projects.

## Success Criteria

1. High-quality visual elements that align with JCC's brand identity, effectively communicate key insights, and support marketing strategies.
2. Collaboration between the Researcher and Designer agents to ensure designs are based on accurate and relevant data from market research.
3. Meeting project deadlines and delivering completed designs on time.
4. Comprehensive analysis of home appliance trends and competitor strategies based on Naver Blog data.
5. Identification of key insights, opportunities, and recommendations for marketing strategies.
6. Well-structured and easy-to-understand report with clear visualizations and actionable recommendations.
