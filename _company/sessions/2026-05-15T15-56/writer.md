### Task Description

6. **Review and Refine Work**: As the agents work on their respective tasks, I will periodically review their progress and provide feedback for improvements if necessary. The goal is to produce high-quality copywriting materials and scripts that meet the company's standards and contribute to its success.

7. **Finalize and Deliver Output**: Once the task has been completed to satisfaction, I will finalize the output and deliver it to the CEO for approval. If necessary, I will work with the agents involved to make any final revisions or adjustments before submitting the completed work.
